## Connect in one step

Add `https://capture.mcp.blencorp.com/mcp` as a custom connector (a remote MCP server) in Claude,
ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code, or another assistant. Your assistant opens a page from the server. Tick the
sources you use, paste your own [key](/keys/) for each, and continue. There is no account to create.
You need at least one key, and a source without a key is simply off. The reference codes and
USAspending need none, but the hosted server will not connect without at least one key (run it
yourself to use only those).

## Ask in plain language

- "Which small business set-aside solicitations were posted in Texas this month?"
- "Who won the most 8(a) sole source awards at the VA in August and September 2025?"
- "Is this vendor excluded from federal contracting, and what have they been awarded?"

## What it covers

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Reference codes" icon="information">
    Official FPDS descriptions for set-aside, competition, and award-type codes. It needs no key.
  </Card>
  <Card title="USAspending" icon="list-format">
    Awards, spending by category, budgetary resources, and groupings by agency, recipient, NAICS,
    PSC, month, or set-aside. It needs no key.
  </Card>
  <Card title="SAM.gov" icon="document">
    Contract opportunities, entity registrations, and exclusions, and two tools that join them
    with USAspending awards.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tango" icon="open-book">
    Contracts, grants, vendor profiles, GAO bid protests, IDVs and vehicles, OTAs, and spending
    over time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="HigherGov" icon="pencil">
    Forecasts, opportunities, awarded contracts for recompete targeting, federal contacts, and
    solicitation documents.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## How it works

<Steps>

1. **Pick the tool.** Your assistant chooses one of 34 narrow tools, such as
   `search_tango_contracts` or `get_sam_opportunities`.
2. **Fill in the arguments.** The server checks each one before anything leaves it.
3. **Get the answer.** The server calls the source with your key, paces the call, and returns
   JSON that says which filters ran and which did not.

</Steps>

The server keeps no database. Your keys travel inside the token your assistant holds, and the server
uses them for your calls only.
